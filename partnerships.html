Dispensary Partnerships

Waived deposits are earned through a real partnership.

Dispensaries can request a waived deposit fee when they agree to partner with Pixie's Pantry, let us use their facility and electricity, help advertise the event, and refer interested customers or patients to us. This page keeps the full partnership rules separate from the homepage so the main pitch stays clean: partner with us for a free show.

Quick answer

  • Dispensary deposit may be waived case-by-case
  • Host must provide space, electricity, and permission
  • Host must advertise, share, and refer people to Pixie's Pantry
  • Pixie's Pantry must be allowed to display cards, flyers, QR codes, and booking info
  • No cannabis, THC, nicotine, tobacco, alcohol, or controlled substances provided by Pixie's Pantry

What the dispensary provides in exchange for a waived deposit

A waived deposit only makes sense when the host location is giving Pixie's Pantry real value in exchange for the time, setup, planning, travel, inventory prep, and opportunity cost.

Facility access

The dispensary provides approved space for the table or build bar, a safe legal event area, reasonable access for setup and teardown, parking/load-in instructions, and permission to be present onsite.

Electricity and basic setup support

The dispensary allows Pixie's Pantry to use electricity when needed for lighting, checkout, signage, displays, or small event equipment. The host should also clarify table, chair, and layout expectations before event day.

Advertising and promotion

The dispensary agrees to promote the Build-A-Bong event through its social media, in-store conversation, email or text list when available, staff reminders, website/event calendar when available, and in-store signage or flyers.

Referrals and warm handoff

The host agrees to refer interested customers, patients, private party hosts, retail owners, and community contacts to Pixie's Pantry for future Build-A-Bong events, hardware education, or private bookings.

Pixie's Pantry must be allowed to display and promote

Business cards and flyers

We must be able to place Pixie's Pantry business cards, flyers, event menus, and booking information at or near our setup.

QR codes and digital links

We must be allowed to display QR codes for booking, shopping, education resources, event interest forms, and Pixie's Pantry contact information.

Educational materials

We must be allowed to share compliant hardware education, safety reminders, setup information, and non-cannabis/non-consumption education materials.

Event photos and content

When allowed by the venue and guests, Pixie's Pantry may capture table setup photos, display photos, and promotional content. Guest privacy and dispensary policies still apply.

Clear naming as event provider

The dispensary should identify Pixie's Pantry as the Build-A-Bong event provider when advertising, posting, referring, or discussing the event.

Future booking visibility

The event should help people understand that Pixie's Pantry can be booked for private parties, retail pop-ups, birthdays, themed workshops, and Mississippi event activations.
