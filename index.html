What This Is

A party, a workshop, and a pop-up build bar in one.

This is not just a table of products. It is a guided, hands-on experience where guests build a personalized 6-inch or 9-inch water pipe setup from real hardware options.

Anyone can host.

You do not have to be a business to book us. A friend group, birthday host, bachelorette group, private party, dispensary, smoke shop, boutique, festival vendor, or local business can bring Pixie's Pantry in as long as the event is booked and paid for.

Guests can pay individually ahead of the event, or the host can pay for the full group. That makes it flexible for ticketed pop-ups, private parties, employee events, and sponsored retail activations.

FAQ

Straight answers before you book.

Use this page to explain the event before someone ever messages you.

Does everyone have to pay together?

No. Guests can pay individually, or one host can pay for the group. We will decide the easiest setup when you book.

How many guests do I need?

Tell us your estimated guest count when you inquire. Smaller private groups and larger pop-ups can both work depending on date, location, and package.

Do you provide cannabis?

No. Pixie's Pantry does not provide cannabis, THC products, controlled substances, or consumption. This is a hardware build and education experience.

Can a dispensary host this?

Yes. Dispensaries and cannabis-adjacent retailers can book a compliant hardware-focused pop-up or build bar experience.

Can I create my own theme?

Yes. You can choose one of our themes or ask us to create a custom theme around your event, colors, brand, holiday, or crowd.

What do guests leave with?

Guests leave with the finished build tied to the package selected for the event, plus basic care and setup guidance.
