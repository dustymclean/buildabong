1. Parties to This Agreement

This Host Agreement and Terms of Service is between Pixie’s Pantry, referred to as “Pixie’s Pantry,” “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our,” and the person, business, venue, organization, or representative booking or hosting the event, referred to as “Host,” “you,” or “your.” Participants, guests, attendees, customers, vendors, and other individuals attending or taking part in the event are also expected to follow all applicable event rules and safety requirements.

2. Event Description

Build-A-Bong events are interactive, hands-on creative workshops where participants assemble, customize, or select glassware, water pipe hardware, accessories, and related components from available inventory and upgrade options.

  • Build-A-Bong and Build-A-Rig workshops.
  • DIY glassware experiences and themed build bars.
  • Hardware education and product education.
  • Retail activations, dispensary pop-ups, smoke shop pop-ups, vendor events, festivals, private parties, birthdays, group events, fundraisers, and community events.

Specific inventory, colors, styles, themes, components, prices, and upgrade options may vary by event.

3. Hardware-Only Statement

Pixie’s Pantry is a hardware, accessories, education, and event company. Pixie’s Pantry does not sell, provide, distribute, gift, handle, transport, or promote the on-site use of cannabis, THC products, controlled substances, nicotine, tobacco, alcohol, or illegal substances at Build-A-Bong events.

Our events are focused on glassware assembly, hardware education, creative customization, responsible product knowledge, design and theme experiences, event entertainment, and retail education.

4. No Consumption Policy

Unless specifically allowed by the venue and all applicable laws, Build-A-Bong events are not consumption events. Participants may not use the event as an opportunity to violate venue rules, state law, local ordinances, federal law, private property rules, or event staff instructions.

Pixie’s Pantry is not responsible for participant conduct before, during, or after an event. If unsafe, unlawful, or inappropriate conduct occurs, Pixie’s Pantry may stop the event, remove participants, or leave the location without refund.

5. Educational Purpose

Pixie’s Pantry may provide general education about hardware, accessories, product types, setup options, cleaning considerations, storage, glassware handling, or event-related products. Pixie’s Pantry does not provide medical advice, legal advice, healthcare recommendations, dosing advice, consumption instructions, diagnosis, treatment plans, or legal compliance opinions.

6. Booking Requirements

A booking is not confirmed until all required steps are complete. A confirmed booking may require a completed booking request, approval by Pixie’s Pantry, confirmed date and time, confirmed location, deposit payment, signed agreement if required, guest count estimate, event package selection, theme selection if applicable, travel approval if applicable, and venue permission if applicable.

Pixie’s Pantry reserves the right to decline any booking request for any lawful reason.

7. Host Responsibilities

The Host agrees to provide accurate booking information, confirm event date, time, and location, secure venue permission, confirm guest count expectations, communicate event rules to guests, provide safe and reasonable event space, provide access for setup and breakdown, ensure guests behave appropriately, pay all required balances on time, notify Pixie’s Pantry of changes as soon as possible, and follow all venue, local, state, and federal rules.

The Host is responsible for any permissions required from the venue, property owner, business owner, landlord, manager, or event organizer.

8. Venue Requirements

The event venue must provide safe, reasonable conditions for setup and participation, including safe access, adequate space, reasonable lighting, stable work surfaces, safe flooring, parking or loading access when possible, protection from severe weather for outdoor events, electricity if agreed or required, and permission for Pixie’s Pantry to operate.

Pixie’s Pantry may refuse setup or stop the event if conditions are unsafe, inaccessible, unauthorized, overcrowded, threatening, illegal, or otherwise unsuitable.

9. Age Requirements

Hosts are responsible for ensuring that all participants meet any applicable age requirements. Some events, products, venues, or themes may require age restrictions. For events involving minors, parent or legal guardian permission or supervision may be required, certain products or components may be restricted, additional waivers may be required, and Pixie’s Pantry may decline minor participation when appropriate.

10. Participant Conduct

Participants must behave respectfully and safely. Harassment, threats, violence, unsafe handling of glass, property destruction, theft, disruptive behavior, illegal activity, intoxicated or unsafe behavior, or refusal to follow staff instructions may result in removal without refund.

11. Inventory Availability

Inventory varies by event. Pixie’s Pantry will make reasonable efforts to provide selected themes, requested colors, build options, and upgrade choices, but specific items are not guaranteed unless confirmed in writing. Pixie’s Pantry may substitute comparable items when needed.

12. Product Variations

Product photos, sample builds, social media examples, catalog pages, flyers, and promotional images are examples only. Actual event inventory may vary in color, size, shape, pattern, accessories, packaging, theme materials, and upgrade availability. Each build experience is unique and small variations do not qualify for refunds.

13. Pricing, Packages & Upgrades

Base event pricing, per-person pricing, dual setup pricing, upgrades, add-ons, travel fees, custom theme fees, and product options may vary by event. Pricing may depend on guest count, build type, theme, location, travel, inventory, upgrade selections, special requests, setup complexity, and event duration.

The Host is responsible for reviewing and approving pricing before the event. Additional upgrades selected during the event must be paid before the participant receives or uses the upgrade.

14. Deposits, Rescheduling & Cancellations

Deposits, refunds, credits, cancellations, rescheduling, no-shows, weather delays, and guest count changes are governed by Pixie’s Pantry’s separate Deposit, Rescheduling & Booking Policy. By booking an event or paying a deposit, the Host agrees to that policy.

Pixie’s Pantry is not responsible for lost, stolen, misplaced, or damaged personal property. Participants are responsible for their own belongings, including phones, bags, wallets, keys, clothing, personal accessories, and completed builds after they are received.

Hosts must provide reasonable access for setup, operation, and breakdown. Delays caused by the Host, venue, crowd, parking, access issues, or lack of preparation may shorten the event time. Pixie’s Pantry is not required to extend the event due to host-caused delays.

18. Travel & Mobile Events

Mobile events may require travel fees based on mileage, drive time, fuel, parking, loading difficulty, staff needs, event duration, and overnight accommodations if needed. If the event location changes after booking, travel fees and availability may change.

22. Safety

Participants must follow all safety instructions provided by Pixie’s Pantry, including handling glass carefully, keeping work areas clear, reporting broken glass immediately, keeping children, pets, or non-participants away from the setup area if needed, and using reasonable care when transporting completed items. Failure to follow safety instructions may result in removal without refund.

23. Assumption of Risk

Participants acknowledge that events involving glassware, accessories, craft materials, tables, tools, packaging, and handling activities involve some risk. By participating, each participant voluntarily assumes the risks associated with normal workshop activities.
