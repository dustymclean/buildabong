## New page added

### partnerships.html

This page explains the dispensary waived-deposit partnership model:

- Dispensaries can request a waived deposit fee case-by-case.
- A waived deposit is not automatic.
- The dispensary must provide real value in exchange for the waived deposit.
- The dispensary should provide facility access, usable space, electricity if needed, and setup permission.
- The dispensary must help advertise the event and refer people to Pixie's Pantry.
- Pixie's Pantry must be allowed to display business cards, flyers, QR codes, booking information, and educational materials.
- Pixie's Pantry remains hardware-only and does not provide cannabis, THC, nicotine, tobacco, alcohol, controlled substances, or on-site consumption.
