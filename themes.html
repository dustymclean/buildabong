How custom theme pricing works

Base event price

Most guests still start from the regular $40-$60 build range or the $65+ dual setup range.

Theme complexity

Extra sourcing, specialty colors, upgraded decor, large displays, rare accents, or custom signage may raise the quote.

Fair final quote

We price based on supplies, prep time, travel, setup time, inventory held, guest count, and whether guests pay individually or as a group.
