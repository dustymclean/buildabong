Pre-shop your Build-A-Bong event.

Browse some of the bases, colorways, downstems, bangers, carb caps, and accessories that may be available for your event. Guests can request favorite colors and build styles ahead of time, including a mixture of glass styles, and Pixie's Pantry will discuss it, price it fairly, and do our best to deliver it.

Inventory disclaimer: requests are welcome, but event stock can vary by supplier availability, event size, travel setup, breakage risk, and what is reserved first. A request is not a guarantee until confirmed.

How to use this page: pick a base, note the colorway, decide whether you want flower-only, dual setup, or a mixture of glass, then submit your color request below or include it with your quote request.
